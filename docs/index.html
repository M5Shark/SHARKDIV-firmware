SHARKDIV flasher

v1.7.28 · flashes straight from the browser, no installs

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on desktop — the flasher needs Web Serial.
  2. Connect SHARKDIV with a USB data cable (not charge-only) and switch it on.
  3. Click Connect & Flash, pick the serial port, and wait. The screen lights up while it flashes — that keeps the board powered.
  4. If it hangs on connect: hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT, then retry.

          Switches off mid-flash, or won't turn on?
          
The board's battery chip turns it off after about 30 seconds when it draws very little power — which is what happens with no firmware running, for example after a flash was cut short. Once the flasher connects it lights the screen, and that keeps the board on.

          
Try this first: tick Erase flash first, plug in USB, switch the board on and click Connect & Flash within about 20 seconds, before it cuts out. When the screen lights up, it will stay on until the flash is done.
